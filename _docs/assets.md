# Assets & Free Resources

Enhance your 3D creations with free, open-source, and public domain 3D models, textures, avatars, and audio. Using community-created assets is a fantastic way to accelerate world-building and support open creation.

---

## Free & Open Source Asset Resources

### 3D Models & Avatars
* **[babylon.js](https://doc.babylonjs.com/toolsAndResources/assetLibraries/):** This section includes a range of libraries for textures, models, and more that you can load into the Playground.
* **[Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/):** Thousands of low-poly 3D models offered under free licenses, ideal for performance-friendly 3D web scenes.
* **[Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/):** A massive repository of downloadable 3D models with filter options for Creative Commons (CC) licensed and free content.
* **[Kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/):** High-quality, public domain (CC0) 2D and 3D game assets, textures, and audio clips with no copyright restrictions.
* **[VRoid Hub](https://hub.vroid.com/):** A showcase platform to discover and download VRM format 3D anime avatars compatible with the engine.

### Textures & Materials
* **[AmbientCG](https://ambientcg.com/):** Hundreds of public domain (CC0) high-resolution Seamless PBR textures, materials, and HDRIs.
* **[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/):** Free 100% CC0 texture sets, 3D models, and HDRIs for realistic lighting and surface materials.

### Audio & Sound Effects
* **[Freesound](https://freesound.org/):** A collaborative database of Creative Commons licensed sound effects, ambient tracks, and audio samples.
* **[OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/):** A community repository for free game audio, music loops, and sound effect packs.

## Creating Your Own Assets

If you prefer to design custom assets or generate original soundscapes, consider these free tools:

* **[Digital Education & Safety Foundation Audio Tools](https://audio.digitaleducationsafety.org/):** An accessible suite of web-based tools designed for creating, editing, and generating custom sound effects and audio assets for your projects.
* **[Blender](https://www.blender.org/):** A powerful, free, and open-source 3D creation suite for modeling, sculpting, animating, and exporting `.gltf` / `.glb` files.
* **[MagicaVoxel](https://ephtracy.github.io/):** A free, lightweight 8-bit voxel art editor for creating 3D blocky models and `.obj` / `.mcstructure` assets.
* **[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/):** A free, open-source, cross-platform audio editor for recording and editing sound files.

## Licensing & Attribution

When using free assets in your projects, acknowledging and attributing the original creators helps sustain open communities and respects creator intellectual property.

### Common Asset Licenses
* **CC0 (Public Domain):** Free to use for any purpose, with or without modification, and without requiring attribution (though giving credit is always appreciated!).
* **CC-BY (Attribution):** Free to use and modify, provided you give appropriate credit to the creator.
* **CC-BY-SA (Attribution-ShareAlike):** Free to use and modify, provided you attribute the author and distribute your work under the same license.

### Giving Credit
To attribute an asset creator in your project documentation, README, or workspace description, include:
1. **Asset Title / Name**
2. **Creator / Author Name** (with a link to their profile or website)
3. **License Type** (with a link to the license text)
4. **Source Link** (where you downloaded the asset)

> **Disclaimer:** *Asset licenses and usage terms are set by individual creators and platform hosts. Users are responsible for verifying license terms, compliance requirements, and restrictions directly on the source website prior to using assets in their projects.*
